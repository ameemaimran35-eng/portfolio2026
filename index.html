ABOUT ME

I’m Ameema Imran, a Full-Stack Developer and Digital Creator currently pursuing my 3-year ADSE / ACCP-AI diploma at Aptech.

My development journey started with the fundamentals of web development and has grown into working with both frontend and backend technologies. I enjoy understanding how a complete application works — from the interface users interact with to the server-side logic, database and APIs working behind the scenes.

I work with technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, PHP, MySQL, Node.js and APIs. I also explore AI-powered development using technologies such as Gemini and Groq APIs.

Alongside development, I have an interest in graphic design, SEO, WordPress and digital marketing, which helps me approach projects from both a technical and creative perspective.

I enjoy building real projects, participating in competitions, learning new technologies and turning ideas into practical digital solutions. My goal is to continue growing as a strong Full-Stack Developer while creating useful, modern and meaningful digital experiences.
